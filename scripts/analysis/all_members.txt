

########## AskCount ##########

--- answered (field) ---
SIG: askCount.answered: number
LEAD: Answers that arrived in time.

--- asks (field) ---
SIG: askCount.asks: number
LEAD: Questions the engine put to this pack.

--- disabled (field) ---
SIG: askCount.disabled: boolean
LEAD: True once the engine has stopped asking this pack this kind at all.

--- late (field) ---
SIG: askCount.late: number
LEAD: Answers that arrived after the engine had acted, and were dropped.

--- missed (field) ---
SIG: askCount.missed: number
LEAD: Deadlines that passed with no answer.

--- skipped (field) ---
SIG: askCount.skipped: number
LEAD: Asks never sent, because this kind is switched off for this pack.


########## Asks ##########

--- kinds (field) ---
SIG: asks.kinds: { [string]: AskCount }
LEAD: The same counters per kind of question, e.g. `kinds["break"].missed`.


########## Bar ##########

SIG: bar.color: BarColor

--- destroy (method) ---
SIG: bar:destroy()

--- progress (field) ---

--- visible (field) ---

LEAD: Whether players see the bar; false takes it off their screens, true or nil shows it again.

########## Biomes ##########

--- define (function) ---
SIG: biomes.define(def: BiomeDefinition) → string
LEAD: Defines a biome, or changes the one of that name, and returns its full name ("mypack:crystal"). Call it at the top of the script: a player learns the biomes when they join.

########## Block ##########

--- behaviour (field) ---
SIG: block.behaviour: string ?
LEAD: The rule Citadel runs for it, such as "grass", "flowing", "torch" or "falling"; nil for a block that does nothing on its own.

--- drops (field) ---
SIG: block.drops: boolean ?
LEAD: The one field a pack writes. Whether a block of this kind that the world breaks by itself leaves its item: true leaves it on a lite world too, false never, nil is the world's default. Reads what this pack set.

--- flammability (field) ---
SIG: block.flammability: { catches: number, burns_away: number }?
LEAD: Fire's two numbers: how readily it catches and how fast it burns away. Nil in a game with no block-update tables.

--- hardness (field) ---
SIG: block.hardness: number
LEAD: Seconds-scale hardness in this version's numbers; below zero is unbreakable.

--- light (field) ---
SIG: block.light: number
LEAD: What it gives off, 0..15, and what a step through it takes away.

--- light_filter (field) ---
SIG: block.light_filter: number
LEAD: What a step through it takes off the light, 0 to 15.

--- material (field) ---
SIG: block.material: Material ?
LEAD: The jar's Material as five questions; nil in a game with no block-update tables.

--- name (field) ---
SIG: block.name: string
LEAD: The block's canonical name.

--- opaque (field) ---
SIG: block.opaque: boolean
LEAD: Whether it is an opaque full cube, which is not the material's question.

--- random_ticks (field) ---
SIG: block.random_ticks: boolean
LEAD: Whether the random-tick pass acts on it.

--- resistance (field) ---
SIG: block.resistance: number ?
LEAD: The three the block-update tables own: nil in a game that has none.

RETURNS: ?


########## BlockAt ##########

--- at (field) ---
SIG: blockAt.at: Vec3
LEAD: The block's position; a live read.

--- become (method) ---
SIG: blockAt:become(entity: string, data: { [string]: any }?)
LEAD: **The door**: this block stops being a block, and that entity appears.
PARAMS: entity:string*, data:{ [ string ]: any }

--- data (field) ---
SIG: blockAt.data: DataView
LEAD: What this block remembers beyond its state; kept with the position, saved with the chunk, and gone when the block goes. Nested writes are tracked; whole-record assignment is supported. Copy before keeping it beyond the hook.

--- destroy (method) ---
SIG: blockAt:destroy()
LEAD: Takes it out, leaving nothing.

--- drop (method) ---
SIG: blockAt:drop(item: ItemSpec, count: number ?)
LEAD: A name, or an item table: a pack item keeps its id.
PARAMS: item:ItemSpec*, count:number

--- facing (field) ---
SIG: blockAt.facing: Facing ?
LEAD: A facing block's front: "north", "south", "west", "east"; nil otherwise. Writable.

--- get (method) ---
SIG: blockAt:get(face: Face) → string ?
LEAD: The block one face away, by name; nil where that chunk is not in memory.
PARAMS: face:Face*
RETURNS: string ?

--- name (field) ---
SIG: blockAt.name: string
LEAD: The block's name with its namespace, such as "rave:crate"; a live read.

--- notify (method) ---
SIG: blockAt:notify()
LEAD: Tells the six neighbours this block changed.

--- powered (field) ---
SIG: blockAt.powered: boolean ?
LEAD: Whether a redstone signal reaches it: what a lamp asks. A live read; nil where the chunk is not loaded.

--- room (field) ---
SIG: blockAt.room: number
LEAD: Which room the block is in.

--- schedule (method) ---
SIG: blockAt:schedule()
LEAD: Asks for this block's `on_tick`, `tick_every` seconds from now.

--- set (method) ---
SIG: blockAt:set(block: BlockRef)
LEAD: Puts another block at this position.
PARAMS: block:BlockRef*

--- state (field) ---
SIG: blockAt.state: number
LEAD: Which of this definition's states it is in, counting from 1. Writable, and bounds-checked against the definition's own `looks` list: it *is* the block state, so a crop's stages cost nothing.


########## BlockDef ##########

--- item (field) ---
SIG: blockDef.item: ItemTable ?
LEAD: The block as an item, when the definition gave it one: `player:give(def.item)`.

--- name (field) ---
SIG: blockDef.name: string
LEAD: Canonical, with the pack's namespace already on it: "rave:crate".

--- states (field) ---
SIG: blockDef.states: number
LEAD: How many states it has; `block.state` counts 1 to this.


########## BlockSpec ##########

--- drops (field) ---
SIG: blockSpec.drops: (Drops | false)?
LEAD: What a break leaves — and, because `on_break` fails closed, what it leaves when this pack is too slow to answer. Never the looks block's. false is nothing, as "none" is.

--- extends (field) ---
SIG: blockSpec.extends: (BlockRef | { [string]: any } | { any })?
LEAD: A handle, a game block's name, a plain table of fields and hooks, or a list of those **innermost first**. There is no class and no metatable: fields merge and hooks wrap.

--- facing (field) ---
SIG: blockSpec.facing: boolean ?
LEAD: Turns its front to whoever places it; `block.facing` reads and turns it.

--- hardness (field) ---
SIG: blockSpec.hardness: number ?
LEAD: Seconds-scale, as vanilla's hardness is; below zero is unbreakable.

--- item (field) ---
SIG: blockSpec.item: (ItemTable | false)?
LEAD: The block in a hand. Its `id` is set to the block's name, so placing it puts this block down, and a break leaves it unless `drops` says. false is no item, also for a block with a texture.

--- label (field) ---
SIG: blockSpec.label: Text ?
LEAD: The name of the item a textured block comes with.

--- light (field) ---
SIG: blockSpec.light: number ?
LEAD: What it gives off, 0 to 15.

--- looks (field) ---
SIG: blockSpec.looks: (string | { string })?
LEAD: A block state the client already draws, because a client has never heard of this pack. One name, or one per state in state order. `|` takes the first this version has: "crimson_planks|nether_brick".

--- material (field) ---
SIG: blockSpec.material: { solid: boolean ?, covers: boolean ?, firm: boolean ?, liquid: boolean ?, burns: boolean ?, }?
LEAD: The jar's Material as five questions: solid, covers (grass under it dies), firm (snow and ice need it), liquid, burns.

--- model (field) ---
SIG: blockSpec.model: (Model | { Model })?
LEAD: Its own shape on 1.21.4+, in place of `texture`: a model that stays inside the block, one for every state or one per state. The block collides as its `looks` block does: "rail" is walked through.

--- name (field) ---
SIG: blockSpec.name: string
LEAD: Without a namespace. The pack's own is put in front of it, and a colon here is refused: a pack cannot write another pack's name.

--- on_added (function) ---
SIG: blockSpec.on_added((block: BlockAt, base: (... any) -> any) -> ()) → ?
LEAD: It has just been stored, whatever stored it.
PARAMS: (block:BlockAt , base: (... any ) -> any ) -> ()*
RETURNS: ?

--- on_break (function) ---
SIG: blockSpec.on_break((block: BlockAt, player: Player ?, by: Breaker, base: (... any) -> any) -> Drops ?) → ?
LEAD: Something broke it. Answers "none", "table" or a drops list; fails closed to the definition's `drops`.
PARAMS: (block:BlockAt , player: Player ?, by: Breaker , base: (... any ) -> any ) -> Drops
RETURNS: ?

--- on_neighbour (function) ---
SIG: blockSpec.on_neighbour((block: BlockAt, from: Vec3, announced: string, base: (... any) -> any) -> ()) → ?
LEAD: A neighbour changed; `from` is its position and `announced` the block name the notifier announced itself as.
PARAMS: (block:BlockAt , from: Vec3 , announced: string , base: (... any ) -> any ) -> ()*
RETURNS: ?

--- on_place (function) ---
SIG: blockSpec.on_place((block: BlockAt, player: Player ?, base: (... any) -> any) -> ()) → ?
LEAD: A player put it down; `block.data` is empty then, ready to be filled.
PARAMS: (block:BlockAt , player: Player ?, base: (... any ) -> any ) -> ()*
RETURNS: ?

--- on_power (function) ---
SIG: blockSpec.on_power((block: BlockAt, powered: boolean, base: (... any) -> any) -> ()) → ?
LEAD: A redstone signal started (`powered` is true) or stopped reaching it. Called once for each, not for every change beside it.
PARAMS: (block:BlockAt , powered: boolean , base: (... any ) -> any ) -> ()*
RETURNS: ?

--- on_random_tick (function) ---
SIG: blockSpec.on_random_tick((block: BlockAt, base: (... any) -> any) -> ()) → ?
LEAD: The random pass landed on it. Not the same thing as `on_tick`, which is why it has the longer name.
PARAMS: (block:BlockAt , base: (... any ) -> any ) -> ()*
RETURNS: ?

--- on_removed (function) ---
SIG: blockSpec.on_removed((block: BlockAt, base: (... any) -> any) -> ()) → ?
LEAD: It has just gone from the world.
PARAMS: (block:BlockAt , base: (... any ) -> any ) -> ()*
RETURNS: ?

--- on_step (function) ---
SIG: blockSpec.on_step((block: BlockAt, body: Body, base: (... any) -> any) -> ()) → ?
LEAD: A body is standing on it; `body` is `{ id, kind }`.
PARAMS: (block:BlockAt , body: Body , base: (... any ) -> any ) -> ()*
RETURNS: ?

--- on_tick (function) ---
SIG: blockSpec.on_tick((block: BlockAt, base: (... any) -> any) -> ()) → ?
LEAD: A scheduled tick this block asked for came due.
PARAMS: (block:BlockAt , base: (... any ) -> any ) -> ()*
RETURNS: ?

--- on_trigger (function) ---
SIG: blockSpec.on_trigger((block: BlockAt, cause: Trigger, base: (... any) -> any) -> ()) → ?
LEAD: It was lit, blasted or burnt; `cause` is "ignite", "explosion" or "fire".
PARAMS: (block:BlockAt , cause: Trigger , base: (... any ) -> any ) -> ()*
RETURNS: ?

--- on_use (function) ---
SIG: blockSpec.on_use((block: BlockAt, player: Player ?, held: string ?, base: (... any) -> any) -> Used ?) → ?
LEAD: The hooks. Writing one is what asks for it — there is no flag beside `on_random_tick` saying the random pass may land here.
PARAMS: (block:BlockAt , player: Player ?, held: string ?, base: (... any ) -> any ) -> Used
RETURNS: ?

--- opacity (field) ---
SIG: blockSpec.opacity: number ?
LEAD: What a step through it takes off the light; 15 is an opaque cube.

--- power (field) ---
SIG: blockSpec.power: (number | { [string]: number } | { number | { [string]: number } })?
LEAD: What it gives a redstone circuit, 0 or 15: one number for every face, a table keyed by face (the names `texture` takes), or a list of either with one entry per state. It is the power a block of redstone gives.

--- resistance (field) ---
SIG: blockSpec.resistance: number ?
LEAD: What an explosion has to chew through.

--- texture (field) ---
SIG: blockSpec.texture: (Faces | { Faces })?
LEAD: Its own picture on 1.21.4+: one for every state, or one per state.

--- tick_every (field) ---
SIG: blockSpec.tick_every: number ?
LEAD: Seconds between `block:schedule()` and `on_tick`; 0.5 unless you say.

--- creative (field) ---

--- tool (field) ---
SIG: breaker.tool: string ?
LEAD: The item in hand, or nil for a bare hand and for the four causes that have none.
