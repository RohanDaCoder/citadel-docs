Citadel/Reference/Player/message

Method of Player

player:message

What shows on this player's screen: the calls ui has, for them alone, plus their own menu and overlay. Sends a chat line to this player alone; color is for the words that did not pick one. Chat is not part of the screen, so it is not in ui.

player:message(text: Text, color: Ink?)

Parameters

textTextrequired
colorInkoptional

Used in

  • player:message("Walk around, then type /spawn to return to the gold block.", "aqua")Guide: Your first pack
  • reads their name; `player.ui.title` shows them a title; `player:message`Guide: Your first pack
  • player:message(`Walk onto the gold plates! Your best is {best}.`, "gold")Guide: Coin rush

  • player:message(`Your best time is {string.format("%.1f s", best)}.`, "green")Guide: Parkour, a room each
