title: 'block.drops'
description: 'The one field a pack writes'
---

Part of [`block`](/ref/Block/).

```luau
block.drops: boolean ?
```

The one field a pack writes. Whether a block of this kind that the world breaks by itself leaves its item: true leaves it on a lite world too, false never, nil is the world's default. Reads what this pack set.
