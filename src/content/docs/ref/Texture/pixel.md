---
title: 'texture:pixel'
description: 'Sets one pixel; (0, 0) is the top left.'
---

Part of [`Texture`](/ref/Texture/).

```luau
texture:pixel(x: number, y: number, color: Ink)
```

Sets one pixel; (0, 0) is the top left.

## Parameters

| Name | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `x` | `number` | yes |
| `y` | `number` | yes |
| `color` | [`Ink`](/ref/Ink/) | yes |
