---

Part of [`entityHandle`](/ref/EntityHandle/).

```luau
entityHandle.flying: boolean
```

Intent, a living body with can_fly only: true flies (no gravity; forward moves it along its look, pitch included), false falls and walks. Setting it true on another body is an error.
