A raw RGB video playing on a picture, from `picture:play`; read `playing` or call `stop`.

## Functions

| Call | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [`stop`](/ref/Video/stop/) | False once it ends (and does not loop) or is stopped. Stops the video. |
