Part of [`Menu`](/ref/Menu/).

```luau
menu:destroy()
```

Takes it down and forgets its callbacks. A Bedrock form cannot be taken back: it stays until the player answers, and the answer then lands nowhere.

## Used in

> "- `menu:destroy()` and a Bedrock pick do not fire `on_close`." — [Menus](/ui/menus/)
