Smooth random numbers for a generator, made by the engine.

Part of the [Terrain generation](/world/terrain-generation/) article.

## Functions

| Call                     | Purpose                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [`new`](/ref/noise/new/) | Returns a noise. `scale` is the size of a hill in blocks (64), and each of the `octaves` (1) adds detail `lacunarity` (2) times finer and `gain` (0.5) times as strong. |
