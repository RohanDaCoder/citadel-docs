---
title: 'picture:draw'
description: 'Draws another screen, canvas or view with its top-left at (x, y), skipping its transparent pixels.'
---

Part of [`Picture`](/ref/Picture/).

```luau
picture:draw(source: Screen | Canvas | View, x: number ?, y: number ?)
```

Draws another screen, canvas or view with its top-left at (x, y), skipping its transparent pixels.

## Parameters

| Name | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `source` | `Screen \| Canvas \| View` | yes |
| `x` | `number` | no |
| `y` | `number` | no |
