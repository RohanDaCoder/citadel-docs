Part of [`Player`](/ref/Player/).

```luau
player:stop_sound(name: string ?)
```

Where their view is from: the camera they look out of, or their eyes. One sound by name, or every sound; 1.13 and later.

## Parameters

| Name | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `name` | `string` | no |

## Used in

> "player:stop_sound("rave.kick") -- one sound by name, or every sound with no name (1.13 and later)" — [Body and movement](/players/movement/)
